About Me

My interest in technology started with simply wanting to understand how things work and how software can be used to solve problems. Over time, that interest grew beyond writing code. I became increasingly interested in the problems behind the technology, how businesses operate, how markets create demand, how supply chains move products, and how well designed systems can make those processes more efficient.

Today, I’m developing my foundation in software development, artificial intelligence, and business, while actively turning what I learn into projects. I enjoy building things from the ground up, experimenting with ideas, and learning by actually creating rather than only studying theory.

I’m still early in that journey, which is exactly what makes it exciting. Every project, internship, course, and problem I work on is another opportunity to turn an idea into something real.
